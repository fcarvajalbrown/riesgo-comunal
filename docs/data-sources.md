# Data sources

Every external source the platform uses or evaluated, with what was actually verified against the live endpoint. An endpoint that could not be reached or whose access terms could not be confirmed is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Nothing here is inferred from a website merely displaying data.

Verification method: each endpoint was requested directly (HTTP status, content type, payload shape inspected). Licence and terms were read from the source's own terms page or ArcGIS item metadata. Verification date for every row: 2026-10-05.

Status values:

- **INGESTED**: an adapter exists and runs on a schedule in the MVP.
- **ADAPTER, NEEDS CREDENTIAL**: adapter written, inactive until the operator configures a credential.
- **EVALUATED, NOT USED**: verified but excluded, reason given.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no machine-readable endpoint confirmed, or terms not confirmed.

## Notes per source

### SENAPRED ArcGIS organisation

SENAPRED publishes its hazard maps (Ley 21.364) and many operational layers as public hosted feature layers on ArcGIS Online, organisation id `i7S5PSnIJAUcWvSE`, listing at `https://services5.arcgis.com/i7S5PSnIJAUcWvSE/ArcGIS/rest/services?f=json`. Several hundred services are listed, many of them survey forms or regional working layers; only layers with a clear national scope and description are used. The ArcGIS item metadata (`https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/<id>?f=json`) of the ingested layers carries **no licence text**, so commercial reuse is UNVERIFIED and must be confirmed in writing with SENAPRED before selling the product. See `data-licensing.md`.

Queries use `where=...&outFields=*&f=geojson&outSR=4326&resultOffset=N&resultRecordCount=N`, paged.

### SINCA

The realtime endpoint returns one object per station (`nombre`, `key`, `latitud`, `longitud`, `comuna`, `red`, `region`, `calificacion` Pública/Privada, `empresa`) with a `realtime` array per parameter (`code` such as `PM25`, `PM10`, `0001`...). Each parameter holds a Google Charts style table: hour, a numeric chart value, a colour, and an HTML tooltip carrying the concentration with units and the hour. The numeric `v` field is not the concentration (observed `v=12` with a tooltip of `2 µg/m³`); the adapter parses the concentration from the tooltip and stores the raw row in provenance. SINCA states that online data is **unvalidated** and cannot be used to verify regulatory compliance; every SINCA record is stored with `validation_status = 'unvalidated'` and shown that way.

Station `comuna` is the station's administrative label, which does not always match its location (stations "Lota rural" and "Lota urbana" are labelled Coronel). The platform assigns stations to comunas spatially.

### DMC

Documentation pages at `https://climatologia.meteochile.gob.cl/application/documentacion/getDocumento/{0..24}` show the URL pattern of every service and that each call carries `usuario` (registered e-mail) and `token` (personal API key). The adapter is implemented against the documented response shape of `getDatosRecientesRedEma` and stays disabled until `DMC_USER` and `DMC_TOKEN` are set. **Blocked on an operator registering for a key.**

### SENAPRED live alerts

DMC weather warnings, by contrast, have an official public feed: the WMO Register of Alerting Authorities lists `https://archivos.meteochile.gob.cl/portaldmc/rss/rss.php` as DMC's CAP alert feed, the channel declares itself public domain, and every CAP document carries polygons (or a centre and radius in km for the islands) per forecast zone. The `dmc_cap` adapter ingests it every 10 minutes; a warning applies to a comuna only when its geometry intersects the comuna boundary, and CAP `Update`/`Cancel` messages close the messages they reference. The WMO register also lists DGA and SHOA as Chilean alerting authorities, without a feed URL.

The `senapred.cl/alertas` page is a single-page app backed by an internal API that SENAPRED does not publish. The platform does not call that API. The `senapred_alertas` adapter instead renders the public page in a headless browser and reads the cards a visitor sees: each card has a title (action, level, scope, reason) and a time in Chile local time, and links to the page of the original declaration, which stays the same through monitoring, modification and cancellation entries and is used as the alert id. The newest card of a thread gives its state; "Se cancela" ends it. The scope (región, provincia, comunas, including mixed scopes such as "la provincia de Arauco y las comunas de ...") is resolved to the union of the matching DPA comuna boundaries, and a comuna sees an alert only when the interiors overlap (neighbours sharing a border do not). Limits: the reader depends on the page layout and stops with an error if no cards are found; threads that fall beyond the fifth listing page keep their last known state; SENAPRED has not confirmed terms for this use. The UI labels these alerts as read from the public page and asks users to confirm on senapred.cl. Municipal manual entry with the official link remains available.

### Schools and health facilities

SENAPRED's `centros_de_educacion` and `centros_de_salud` layers turned out to hold 23 and 6 features (buffer extracts), so the national catalogues are taken from the IDE Chile GeoServer node instead. The WFS supports `bbox=xmin,ymin,xmax,ymax,EPSG:4326` (lon/lat order with the short CRS code) and `count`/`startIndex` paging; `sortBy=id` is rejected.

### Rate limits observed

SENAPRED's ArcGIS Online organisation returns error 429 ("API calls for large geometry quota exceeded ... maximum allowed (60) per Minute") when comuna boundaries are paged quickly. The adapter pages boundaries 20 at a time with `maxAllowableOffset=0.00005` and waits 62 s on a 429. DGA's ArcGIS 10.21 MapServer supports only `f=json` (no GeoJSON) and no paging; the adapter converts Esri points itself.

### ICFSR

Field names `ic_ot`, `ic_cc`, `ic_soc`, `ic_gob` are mapped to SENAPRED's four published dimensions (Ordenamiento Territorial; Cambio Climático y Recursos Naturales; Condiciones Socioeconómicas y Demográficas; Gobernanza) by their abbreviations; SENAPRED's layer does not label them. The index is a municipal self-diagnosis (41 variables), per SENAPRED's "Factores Subyacentes" page.

### INE Censo 2024 blocks

The adapter `ine_censo2024` reads only aggregate counts per block or rural entity, never microdata. Checked when it was added: the blocks whose representative point lies in Lota add up to 39,782 persons in 875 polygons, against INE's comuna total of 39,980 in the `Comunal_CPV24` layer (99.5%); Retiro 22,184 in 284 polygons against 22,310 (99.4%). The platform shows the block-based estimate and keeps the official comuna total beside it. INE publishes the same base as downloadable files (GeoParquet) from the census results site; the service was used because it can be filtered by area.

### SHOA SNAM adapter (`shoa_snam`)

The SNAM page at `snamchile.cl` is a plain server-rendered table, not a single-page app, so it is read with one HTTP request and no browser. Each row is one event: its bulletin numbers (from the "Ver Boletín" links), the bulletin times and states (several bulletins of one event are separated by line breaks in the same cell, oldest first), and the epicentre, magnitude and magnitude agency from the "Ver" map link. The bulletin detail behind each number is loaded by the page's own script and is not called.

Each event becomes one alert, `hazard = tsunami`, issuer "SHOA (SNAM)", id `snam-<first bulletin number>`. The state of the newest bulletin sets the level: a state containing "cancel" closes the alert at that bulletin's time, "alarma" gives `Alarma`, "precaución" or "alerta" gives `Alerta`, "informativo" gives `Informativo` and closes the alert at issue (an informative bulletin states there is no threat to the Chilean coast), and any other wording gives `Alerta` so that an unknown state is shown, not hidden. Only "INFORMATIVO PARA LAS COSTAS DE CHILE" was observed on 2026-10-06; the other words are matched by keyword because the exact wording of non-informative states was not seen live. The full state text is kept as the title and in `properties.state`.

Coverage: a SNAM bulletin speaks of the Chilean coast, with no comuna list in the table, so the alert is attached to the comunas whose boundary intersects SENAPRED's tsunami evacuation areas or meeting points already in the database (on 2026-10-06: Arauco, Chanco, Cobquecura, Constitución, Coronel, Curepto, Licantén, Lota, Paredones, Pelluhue, Vichuquén). Inland comunas do not see it. A comuna without the SENAPRED tsunami layer loaded is not covered; the adapter warns when no coastal comuna is found.

Limits: the page answers 403 to agents that do not start with `Mozilla/5.0`, so the adapter sends `Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; <platform agent>)`, which still names the platform. This is an access filter, not a published term, and SHOA has not confirmed terms for automated reading. The reader depends on the table layout and stops with an error when no rows are found. Verified with `python -m app.cli ingest shoa_snam`: 10 records, 0 warnings.

### Filtering and coverage

- **GDACS**: an event is kept when GDACS lists Chile (`CHL` in `gdacs:iso3` or "Chile" in `gdacs:country`), or when it has no country and its point lies within 1 degree of a tenant comuna's bounding box (offshore earthquakes). Its area is the GDACS `bbox`. On 2026-10-06 the feed held 143 items (109 wildfires, 14 floods, 12 droughts, 5 tropical cyclones, 3 earthquakes; 139 Green, 4 Orange), none for Chile, so the live ingestion stored 0 alerts. Each ingestion is a snapshot: a stored GDACS alert that is no longer in the filtered feed is ended.
- **PTWC/NTWC**: a message is kept when its epicentre lies between 120 W and 65 W and between 60 S and 5 N (off South America's Pacific coast) or when its text bulletin names Chile. It is attached to the comunas whose boundary intersects a SENAPRED tsunami evacuation area (7 of the 31 installed tenants on 2026-10-06; all comunas if the layer is not loaded). Later messages of the same event supersede earlier ones, and a cancellation ends the thread. On 2026-10-06 the PHEB feed held a Caribbean information statement (Puerto Rico region, M5.2) and the PAAQ feed an Alaska information statement (M5.3), so the live ingestion stored 0 alerts.
- **WMO Severe Weather**: the aggregated JSON held 1,517 items on 2026-10-06, none from Chile: no `capURL` prefix for a Chilean member and no item text mentioning Chile or the DMC, while the DMC's own CAP RSS (ingested as `dmc_cap`) listed 10 bulletins at the same time. It therefore adds nothing for Chile today, neither as a mirror nor as a fallback, and no adapter was written. If Chile appears in it later, its items would mirror the DMC CAP feed already ingested.
- The PTWC text bulletin is fetched only from the link the Atom entry publishes. No undocumented API is called.
